BHF Survivor Pool

Wireframes of the screens in the app today. Names and weeks are the sample pool used by the Xcode previews. A future pick stays off the Pool tab until that game kicks off.

Sign in with Apple→ Claim a PIN→ My Entries→ Pick a team and Pool standings Admins also get Commissioner queues
Tabs Pool My Entries Pick editor Commissioner Pieces

Tabs

The app opens on Pool. After sign-in it switches to My Entries. Commissioner appears only for an admin account.

Pool

PoolBoardView. Signed-in players see public picks only, for weeks that have already kicked off.

My Entries

Sign in, claim a PIN, then the list of entries on this Apple account. Sign Out stays in the toolbar.

Pick editor

EntryEditorView. One team per season. The spread is a label. The save bar stays on screen.

Commissioner

Queues when the search is empty. A name search switches the list to matching entries. Close week asks before it applies.

Pieces

The controls those screens share. EntryPickRules is what the labels say: available, used in another week, or locked after kickoff.
